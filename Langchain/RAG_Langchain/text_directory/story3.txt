3. The Boy Who Refused to Lose

Everyone told Raghav he was “average.”

Average grades. Average talent. Average future.

So he made a deal with himself:

“I won’t try to be extraordinary. I’ll just refuse to quit.”

While others chased motivation, he built discipline.

While others waited for the “right time,” he started anyway.

Years passed. No dramatic success. No overnight breakthrough.

Then one day, people started asking:

“How did you do it?”

Raghav smiled.

There was no secret.

Just thousands of days where quitting would have been easier—but wasn’t chosen.
