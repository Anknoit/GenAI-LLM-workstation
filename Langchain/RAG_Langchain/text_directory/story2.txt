2. The Last Signal

In 2043, satellites had gone silent.

No GPS. No internet. No global communication.

Except one.

A faint, repeating signal—origin unknown.

Mira, a telecom engineer, tracked it using a decaying ground station no one had touched in years. The signal wasn’t random. It was structured. Almost like… a heartbeat.

She decoded it.

Coordinates.

Deep ocean.

She hesitated. Governments dismissed it. “Noise,” they said.

But she knew better.

She took a private vessel and followed the signal.

At the coordinates, the ocean was still. Too still.

Then her system connected automatically.

A message appeared:

“You built networks to connect yourselves. We built one to find you.”

The signal stopped.

Something beneath the water started moving.
