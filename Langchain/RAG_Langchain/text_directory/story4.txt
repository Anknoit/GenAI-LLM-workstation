4. The Silent City

Every night at exactly 2:17 AM, the city went silent.

No cars. No fans. No dogs barking. Not even the hum of electricity.

For 60 seconds.

People called it a glitch. A power anomaly.

Except one girl, Naina.

She stayed awake one night, watching.

At 2:17, everything stopped.

Except her.

And in that silence, she heard footsteps.

Not outside.

Inside the walls.

Something moved through the city when time paused.

And it knew who was awake.
