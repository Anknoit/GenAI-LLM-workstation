1. The Man Who Measured Time

Arjun bought an old pocket watch from a street vendor who claimed it “didn’t measure time—it revealed it.”

At first, it seemed broken. The second hand moved unpredictably—sometimes slow, sometimes skipping ahead.

But then Arjun noticed something unsettling.

Whenever he hesitated in life—whether to speak, to act, to decide—the watch slowed down. When he acted with certainty, it sped up.

One day, during a critical moment at work, he froze. The watch stopped.

Completely.

For the first time, silence filled not just the room—but his mind.

And he realized something terrifying:
Time doesn’t stop when you wait.

It abandons you.

He made his decision instantly.

The watch ticked again.
